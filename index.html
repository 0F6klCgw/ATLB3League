How Scoring Works

Two documents run the league. The Points Sheet is your scorecard for the night. The Points Deck is the pool of extra goals you can pull and claim on top of it. Both are pulled live from Google Sheets below, so what you see here is always current.

Step One

Play your three games

Each league night is three matches. Grab a Points Sheet, write your name at the top, and keep it next to you at the table.

Step Two

Mark what you earned

After each game, tick every line you hit. Standard points are always live. Rotating points change regularly — check them before you build. Bad Guy points subtract.

Step Three

Claim your deck cards

Cards you pull from the Points Deck are extra goals to chase — worth switching decks for. Take the card back to the counter after the round to claim it.

FAQ

When and where does the league meet?
Mondays at Dice City. The event starts at 6pm, but players frequently arrive early to test decks.
Can I join partway through a season?
Yes, you can join any time during the season. Your bottom three scores each season are dropped, so if you miss three weeks those zeroes are the ones that get dropped.
Do I have to play the same deck every week?
No — you can switch decks between rounds, and you may want to based on the points cards you pull from the Points Deck.
How do I claim a Points Deck goal?
Take the card back to the counter after the round.
How often do the rotating points change?
The rotating points change as new sets are released.
